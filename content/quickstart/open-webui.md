With [pinggy.io](https://pinggy.io) you can share your Open WebUI instance from localhost with just a single command. **Replace the port 3000 with the port where Open WebUI is running.**

Pinggy will give you a URL like `https://ranlkfsbjkxry.a.pinggy.link`. Use this URL to access the interface.
