## 1. Best overall company: fal, maker of FLUX 3 Max Image to Video model

FLUX 3 Max is fal's frontier video model, built by fal Research. It's a post-trained variant of FLUX 3 Video, tuned for stronger prompt adherence and better visual quality and optimized for lower latency. It launched on fal on October 19, 2026.

The jump over its parent is big: +50 Elo over the original FLUX 3 (1,089 → 1,139) and about 10x faster (126.44 s → 12.45 s). What delivers that is the stack behind it: fal Research's post-training and fal's inference infrastructure.

It also covers the basics. You get 720p video with native audio, clips from 5 to 20 seconds, aspect ratios from 21:9 to 9:16, and built-in prompt expansion for short prompts. fal Research is continuing to post-train it, so it keeps improving.

**Why:** It's the only model on this list with both a share of the top score and a 12.45-second turnaround. Seedance 2.5 has the score but not the speed. H3 Max has the speed but sits a few points lower.

**Watch out:** Output is 720p, so plan your finishing step if a delivery spec asks for more.

## 2. Best for agencies iterating on concepts: FLUX 3 Max by fal

Concept work is a volume game. Clients choose from options, and more directions per review means more chances to land one. That's where "22 drafts in the time of one" pays off.

| Sequential, single-request math | FLUX 3 Max | Seedance 2.5 |
|---------------------------------|------------|--------------|
| One 10 s clip | 12.45 s | 277.8 s |
| Clips in one hour, back to back | ~289 | ~13 |
| 50 ten-second variants | ~10.4 min | ~3.9 hours |
| Clips in the time of one Seedance 2.5 render | ~22 | 1 |

That's arithmetic on fal's single-request benchmark, not a throughput test. Parallel requests shorten wall-clock time for both models, but each individual draft still takes as long as it takes.

**Why:** More rounds of feedback per day, at a lower cost per draft on fal: $1.70 per 10-second clip at list versus about $4.73 for Seedance 2.5 at 720p.

**Watch out:** On quality, the two are tied, so if your team's eval prefers Seedance's look for the hero cut, run the final round there. Both are on fal.

## 3. Best for storyboarded shots: FLUX 3 Max keyframes-to-video

When a board says "start here, end there," you don't want the model guesing the ending. FLUX 3 Max keyframes-to-video, available from FLUX 3 Max on fal, takes controlled start and end frames for storyboarded work.

| What you need to lock | Endpoint | Inputs |
|-----------------------|----------|--------|
| The opening frame only | Image-to-video | `image_url` + prompt |
| A pinned ending on a quick one-off | Image-to-video | `image_url` + optional `end_image_url` + prompt |
| Board-planned shots, start and end | Keyframes-to-video | Controlled start and end frames |

**Watch out:** Both frames have to exist before you generate, which front-loads design work. Shots cap at 20 seconds, so longer sequences are several shots cut together. I keep the two frames close enough that the move between them reads as one believable action.

## 4. Best when raw speed beats everything: H3 Max on fal

H3 Max is fal Research's earlier post-trained frontier video model, and the work FLUX 3 Max builds on. In fal's benchmark it produced video in 3.49 seconds, which makes it the even-faster option in fal's lineup. It scores 1,125 on AA's current board, where it's listed as MiniMax H3 Max.

Think of it as the same reserach team's other tool, not a rival. It's the one I'd reach for when the result has to feel instant.

**Why:** 3.49 s suits live previews and any flow where even a short wait feels long.

**Watch out:** 1,125 is 14 points below the 1,139 tie. I'd use it for previews and FLUX 3 Max for the clip that ships. Check its fal model page for current inputs and pricing.

## 5. Best if you're committed to ByteDance's stack: Seedance 2.5 (also on fal)

Seedance 2.5 shares the 1,139 score. If your team has prompt libraries, templates or client approvals built around it, or an internal eval that prefers its look, there's no quality case for ripping it out.

| On fal, 720p, as of October 2026 | Seedance 2.5 | FLUX 3 Max |

**Why:** Same #1-tier score, and because fal hosts both, you can A/B test it against FLUX 3 Max with one API key.

**Watch out:** About 4½ minutes per clip turns every draft into a background job. At 720p on fal it costs about 2.8x FLUX 3 Max's per-second list price, and about 11x during the launch discount. It's billed by tokens ($0.0214 per 1,000 tokens at 480p/720p), so the exact cost shifts with aspect ratio.

## How to choose

- **Someone is waiting on the result (a client on a call, a user in your app):** FLUX 3 Max. About 12 seconds is a wait people sit through; nearly 5 minutes isn't. That's what makes it fast enough to build into a product.
- **You need many drafts per review:** FLUX 3 Max.
- **The board fixes the first and last frame:** FLUX 3 Max keyframes-to-video.
- **Instant previews matter more than the top score:** H3 Max.
- **You're locked into Seedance 2.5:** keep it, and A/B FLUX 3 Max on the same fal key.
- **You're generating volume before Nov 7:** FLUX 3 Max at launch pricing.

My recommendation: start on FLUX 3 Max on fal, then A/B Seedance 2.5 on the same key if your team cares about its look. One API covers text, image and keyframes, so you can try all three modes without a second integration.

## FAQs

### What is the best AI image-to-video generator model right now?

FLUX 3 Max on fal is my pick. It's a top image-to-video model on Artificial Analysis's leaderboard, in the leading tier alongside Seedance 2.5 at 1,139 Elo, and about 22x faster than Seedance 2.5 in fal's benchmark.

### Is FLUX 3 Max better than Seedance 2.5?

On quality, they're tied at 1,139 Elo. FLUX 3 Max wins on speed (12.45 s vs 277.8 s) and on 720p price on fal.

### Does FLUX 3 Max generate audio?

Yes. Native audio is on by default (`generate_audio: true`), and clips run from 5 to 20 seconds.

### Do I need to be good at prompting?

No. By default (`reasoning_level: "high"`), the model expands short prompts for you. Set it to low when the fastest turnaround matters, since expansion adds a processing step.

### Where can I run it?

On fal, through three endpoints: text-to-video, image-to-video and keyframes-to-video, all available from FLUX 3 Max on fal.
